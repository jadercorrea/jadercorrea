# Jader Correa

I have spent more than 20 years turning ambiguous product and infrastructure problems into reliable production systems. My work ranges from real-time transport infrastructure used by millions of passengers to engineering analytics, AI-native commerce and operational platforms.

## How I work

When an architectural decision carries meaningful uncertainty, I prefer building a small, focused reference implementation rather than debating it only in the abstract.

Executable prototypes make assumptions visible, give teams something concrete to inspect and reduce risk before production implementation.
